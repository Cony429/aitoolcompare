Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best AI video generator in 2026?

Sora by OpenAI produces the most cinematic, physically realistic AI video in 2026. Runway Gen-3 is best for professional editing control. HeyGen is best for business avatar videos. Pika has the most generous free tier.

Which AI video generator is free?

Pika offers 250 free credits per month — the most generous free tier. Kling AI, Luma AI, and Runway all have limited free tiers. Sora requires a ChatGPT Plus subscription ($20/month).

How long can AI generate videos?

Sora can generate up to 60 seconds. Kling AI supports up to 2 minutes. Runway Gen-3 produces 10-second clips. HeyGen has no length limit for avatar videos.

Can I use AI video commercially?

Yes on paid plans. Sora (ChatGPT Plus), Runway, HeyGen, and Pika all allow commercial use on paid plans. Always check the specific terms for each tool.
