We use Google Analytics (provided by Google LLC) to understand how visitors interact with our website. This service collects anonymous usage data including:

This data is aggregated and anonymized — we cannot identify individual users from it. Google Analytics data is processed under Google's Privacy Policy and Google's data processing terms.
