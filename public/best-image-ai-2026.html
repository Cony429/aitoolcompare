Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best AI image generator in 2026?

Midjourney remains the best for artistic quality and cinematic results. DALL-E 3 (via ChatGPT) is best for accurate text within images. For free unlimited generation, Stable Diffusion run locally is unbeatable.

Which AI image generator is free?

Leonardo.ai offers 150 free credits daily with no watermarks. Stable Diffusion is completely free when run locally. Canva AI has a free tier. DALL-E 3 is accessible free through ChatGPT's limited free plan.

What is the difference between Midjourney and DALL-E 3?

Midjourney produces more artistic, cinematic results but costs $10/month with no free tier. DALL-E 3 (built into ChatGPT Plus) is better at text rendering inside images and photorealistic portraits.

Can AI image generators be used commercially?

Adobe Firefly outputs are fully commercially safe. Midjourney and DALL-E 3 allow commercial use on paid plans. Stable Diffusion outputs can be used commercially depending on the base model's license.
