2026년 올바른 AI 도구 선택 방법

With hundreds of AI tools available, picking the right one comes down to your specific use case. For writing and content creation, Claude and Jasper lead the field. For coding, GitHub Copilot and Cursor are the top choices. For image generation, Midjourney produces the most artistic results while DALL-E 4 excels at accurate text rendering.

If budget is a concern, all of the following offer genuinely useful free tiers: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity AI, Leonardo.ai, ElevenLabs, Suno, GitHub Copilot, and Canva AI. You can build a comprehensive AI toolkit for $0 using free plans alone.

For video generation, Sora leads on cinematic quality while Runway Gen-4 offers the most professional editing control. For business use, HeyGen is the top choice for AI avatar videos.
